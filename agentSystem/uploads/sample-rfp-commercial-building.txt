REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP)
Project: Commercial Building Paint and Coating Services

CLIENT INFORMATION
Organization: Metro Infrastructure Ltd.
Project Name: City Plaza Commercial Complex
Location: Downtown Business District
Contact: procurement@metroinfra.com

PROJECT OVERVIEW
We are seeking proposals for comprehensive paint and coating services for our new commercial building project. The facility consists of a 10-story office complex with retail spaces on the ground floor.

SCOPE OF WORK

1. EXTERIOR WORKS
   - Exterior wall painting (approximately 15,000 sq ft)
   - Weather-resistant emulsion coating
   - Primer application for all exterior surfaces
   - Waterproofing treatment for exposed areas
   - Metal structure rust protection and enamel coating

2. INTERIOR WORKS
   - Office spaces (8 floors, approx. 80,000 sq ft)
   - Interior wall primer and emulsion
   - Ceiling painting with smooth finish
   - Common areas and corridors
   - Retail space painting (ground floor, 10,000 sq ft)

3. SPECIAL REQUIREMENTS
   - Wood surfaces: Varnish and protective coating
   - Metal surfaces: Anti-rust primer and enamel
   - High-traffic areas: Washable and durable finish
   - Fire-resistant coating where required

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Paint Quality Requirements:
- Low VOC, eco-friendly products preferred
- Weather resistance: Minimum 5-year warranty
- Coverage: As per manufacturer specifications
- Colors: As per approved color scheme (to be provided)

Surface Preparation:
- Cleaning, filling, and smoothing all surfaces
- Primer application mandatory
- Multiple coats as required for even finish
